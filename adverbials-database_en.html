Adverbials: valencies, constructions, systemic relations

The database describes morphosyntactic and semantic properties of Russian expressions derived from abstract nouns. These expressions can function as adverbials, predicates, and derived prepositions. Particular attention is given to subject control: whether the subject of an adverbial is coreferential with the subject of the main predicate.

Fields and notation

Source values and codes are preserved. “Preposition / marker” contains prepositions and other markers. АДВ means adverbial, СК means predicate, and ПРЕДЛ means preposition. For subject control, “1 Сб” indicates matching subjects and “2 Сб” indicates different subjects. Compound and uncertain markers (such as “1 Сб / 2 Сб” and “?”) are separate choices.

“Origin” combines derivation information and the source word: for example, “отглаг бегать/бежать” means “a deverbal formation from бегать/бежать”. “No data” indicates an empty value; “Source error” indicates an Excel error without usable text.
